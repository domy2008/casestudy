# Acme Corp — HR Compensation & Leave Policy (2024)

This document describes Acme Corp's employee compensation grades and the
company leave policy. It is intended for all full-time employees and is
maintained by the People Operations team.

## Salary Grades

Base annual salaries are set by grade. Each grade has a defined band; new
hires normally start at the band minimum and progress toward the midpoint
through annual reviews.

| Grade | Role Level            | Minimum (USD) | Midpoint (USD) | Maximum (USD) |
| ----- | --------------------- | ------------- | -------------- | ------------- |
| G1    | Associate             | 55,000        | 62,500         | 70,000        |
| G2    | Professional          | 68,000        | 78,000         | 88,000        |
| G3    | Senior Professional   | 86,000        | 99,000         | 112,000       |
| G4    | Lead / Manager        | 110,000       | 128,000        | 146,000       |
| G5    | Senior Manager        | 140,000       | 162,000        | 184,000       |
| G6    | Director              | 178,000       | 205,000        | 232,000       |

Salary increases take effect on the first pay period of April each year.
Promotions between grades are reviewed twice a year, in March and September.

## Annual Leave

Paid annual leave is granted based on length of service:

| Years of Service | Annual Leave Days |
| ---------------- | ----------------- |
| 0 – 2 years      | 18 days           |
| 3 – 5 years      | 22 days           |
| 6 – 9 years      | 26 days           |
| 10+ years        | 30 days           |

Unused annual leave of up to 5 days may be carried over into the next calendar
year and must be used by March 31. Any balance above the carry-over limit is
forfeited.

## Sick Leave

Employees are entitled to 10 paid sick days per calendar year. A medical
certificate is required for any sick absence longer than 3 consecutive days.
Sick leave does not carry over between years.

## Parental Leave

Primary caregivers are entitled to 16 weeks of paid parental leave. Secondary
caregivers are entitled to 4 weeks of paid parental leave. Parental leave must
be taken within 12 months of the birth or adoption of a child.

## How to Request Leave

All leave requests are submitted through the HR portal and must be approved by
the employee's direct manager at least 5 working days in advance, except for
sick leave, which may be reported on the day of absence.
