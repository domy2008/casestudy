# Acme Corp — Finance Expense & Reimbursement Policy (2024)

This document sets out the rules for business expenses and how employees are
reimbursed. It applies to all employees and contractors who incur costs on
behalf of Acme Corp. It is maintained by the Finance team.

## General Principles

- Expenses must be reasonable, necessary, and directly related to Acme Corp
  business.
- Every claim must include an itemized receipt. Claims without a valid receipt
  are rejected.
- Expenses must be submitted within 30 days of being incurred. Claims older
  than 60 days will not be reimbursed.

## Daily Meal Allowances

The following per-day limits apply when travelling for business. Amounts are
in USD and cover the employee only.

| Travel Type          | Breakfast | Lunch | Dinner | Daily Cap |
| -------------------- | --------- | ----- | ------ | --------- |
| Domestic travel      | 15        | 25    | 40     | 80        |
| International travel  | 20        | 35    | 55     | 110       |

Alcohol is not reimbursable and must be excluded from meal receipts.

## Travel & Accommodation

- Economy class airfare is reimbursed for all flights under 6 hours. Flights of
  6 hours or more may be booked in premium economy with prior manager approval.
- Hotel stays are reimbursed up to 200 USD per night for domestic travel and
  300 USD per night for international travel.
- Ground transport (taxi, rideshare, public transit) is reimbursed at cost.
  Personal vehicle mileage is reimbursed at 0.65 USD per mile.

## Approval Thresholds

| Expense Amount (USD) | Required Approver          |
| -------------------- | -------------------------- |
| Up to 200            | Direct manager             |
| 201 – 1,000          | Department head            |
| 1,001 – 5,000        | Finance director           |
| Over 5,000           | CFO                        |

## How to Submit a Claim

Submit expense claims through the Finance portal with itemized receipts
attached. Approved claims are reimbursed in the next payroll cycle, normally
within 10 business days of approval. Questions about a reimbursement should be
directed to the Finance team.
